Editable Invoice Total

Match a vendor bill's Total to the number on the paper invoice - without hunting through every line.

The problem

A supplier's invoice sometimes shows a total that doesn't quite match what you entered from the packing slip or purchase order - a rounding difference, a fee folded into one line, a discount applied at the bottom. Odoo's Total field on a vendor bill is read-only: it's a sum of the lines, not something you can just overwrite. So you go back and force one of the lines to a number that isn't really its price, just to make the totals agree.

How it works

1

Open a draft vendor bill

Click the pencil icon next to the Total amount.

2

Type the real total

Enter the amount from the supplier's invoice and confirm.

3

Done

A single correction line absorbs the difference. Your itemized lines are untouched.

Built to stay safe

Your VAT stays correct

The correction line is untaxed. Tax amounts on your real invoice lines are never recalculated or touched.

Draft-only, by design

The Total is only editable while the bill is still a draft - exactly where the native Total field would otherwise sit read-only. Nothing changes about a posted invoice.

Cleans up after itself

Set the Total back to the original sum of the lines and the correction line is removed automatically - no manual tidy-up.

Where it applies

Vendor bills, vendor credit notes, and vendor receipts (in_invoice, in_refund, in_receipt) in draft state. Requires the Invoicing / Accounting app. No new models, no extra configuration, no security rules to set up - install it and the pencil icon shows up where it's relevant.
